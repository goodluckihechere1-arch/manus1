07Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you reach out.

What areas does Tap Plumbing Corp serve?

The company lists the Bronx, Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn, and Westchester County. Contact the office to confirm service availability for a specific property address.

Do you handle commercial and residential plumbing?

Yes. The company lists both commercial and residential plumbing, alongside construction, renovation, gas piping, fixture, repair, water heater, and boiler work.

Can you work on new construction projects?

New construction plumbing is one of the company's listed services. Share the project location, stage, and scope to begin a discussion.

Can property managers request commercial plumbing estimates?

Yes. Property managers can use the estimate form or contact the office by phone or email to discuss a commercial property and its scope.

Do you coordinate permits and inspections?

The company's commercial and new-construction pages describe NYC DOB permit and inspection coordination or filing where applicable. Contact the team to discuss requirements for a specific project.

How can I request a plumbing estimate?

Call 718-337-8749, email Office@tapplumbingcorp.com, or use the estimate form. The form on this redesign concept prepares an email draft; it does not submit a request directly to the company.

What information should I provide about my project?

Include the property location, type of work, approximate scope, whether it is an urgent repair or a planned project, and the best way to contact you. For commercial or construction work, include any useful project or coordination details.
